FEATURED PROJECT

SpeakEase Connect

A proposed accessible communication mobile app

SpeakEase Connect is a proposed app designed to support people who experience communication barriers, including people with speech difficulties, hearing impairments, and blind or low-vision users.

The concept brings helpful communication tools together in one place, making it easier to type, speak, select ready-made messages, and access voice guidance.
